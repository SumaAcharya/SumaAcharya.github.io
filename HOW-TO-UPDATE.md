# How to update your website

Your site: https://sumaacharya.github.io

## Add a Tech Blog post
1. Open this link (it opens a ready-made template): https://github.com/SumaAcharya/SumaAcharya.github.io/new/main/_posts?filename=2026-09-24-my-post-title.md&value=---%0Atitle%3A%20%22Your%20post%20title%22%0Adescription%3A%20One%20line%20summary%20shown%20on%20the%20homepage.%0A---%0A%0AStart%20writing%20here.%20Use%20%23%23%20for%20headings%2C%20%2A%2Abold%2A%2A%2C%20and%20add%20images%20like%20%21%5Bcaption%5D%28/whimsicature/your-image.jpg%29.%0A
2. Change the file name to `YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md` (use today's date).
3. Edit the title and description, and write your post below the dashes.
4. Click **Commit changes**. It appears in the Tech Blog section in 1–2 minutes.

To list a post published elsewhere (Medium, LinkedIn…), add `external_url: https://...` under `description`.

## Add caricatures to Whimsicature
1. Open https://github.com/SumaAcharya/SumaAcharya.github.io/upload/main/whimsicature
2. Drag in your images (.jpg, .png or .webp), then click **Commit changes**.

The file name becomes the caption: `chai-and-chaos.jpg` shows as "Chai and chaos".

## Add your photo
Upload a square photo named `profile.jpg` to https://github.com/SumaAcharya/SumaAcharya.github.io/upload/main/assets
It appears as a polaroid in the top section.

## Update your CV
Your CV page is `cv.html`. Open https://github.com/SumaAcharya/SumaAcharya.github.io/edit/main/cv.html, change the text, and commit.
Visitors can save it as a PDF with the "Download / Print PDF" button.

## Optional: tags on a post
Add `tags: [batteries, circuits]` under `description` to show tags on the post.
